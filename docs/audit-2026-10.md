# Barrowfall: full audit (2026-10-22)

The question: does it feel premium, is it fun, does it make people come back
and grind, and is it neither repetitive nor too easy?

This audit is based on:

- **Autopilot runs:** 40 hunts across 4 heroes × 3 tiers × hero levels 1/10/20
  (`--bot`).
- **Screenshots:** six screens at iPhone size, all looked at.
- **Code and data:** a read of every tuning table in `db.gd` and the economy in
  `game.gd`.

The autopilot plays without gear and without spending talents, so its
Veteran/Nightmare numbers are a floor, not the real experience. Its Normal
numbers are close to what a new player sees.

---

## The short version

The game has more *systems* than most mobile games of this size: 5 modes,
talents, gear tiers, skills, cards and evolutions, pacts, omens, clues, a
renown road and bounties. What it lacks is the stuff that makes those systems
*feel* good, and a difficulty curve that makes them *matter*.

The ten things that matter most, in order:

1. **Normal is a walkover and over in about 80 seconds.** Every Normal hunt was
   won, mostly with health to spare. The boss died 15–35 s after waking.
   First impressions are "too easy".
2. **Nightmare is a wall for ranged heroes.** Mage and Ranger died before the
   boss in 7 of 8 Nightmare runs, *even at hero level 20*. Ten extra hero
   levels changed nothing.
3. **Shards gate progress about 8× harder than gold.** Gold piles up with
   nothing to buy. Shards land at random on heroes you aren't playing.
4. **The first five minutes look cheap.** The "Knight" on the camp screen is a
   bald man in a tunic holding a dagger. A new player's hero looks like a
   villager.
5. **The win moment is flat.** On Victory, loot labels are tiny and stacked on
   top of each other far from the hero, and the combat buttons stay up. The
   results screen has no "Play again", and half of it is empty.
6. **Scenery hides the action.** The gallows prop sits in front of the boss,
   and on the death shot a foreground rock covers a third of the screen.
7. **One enemy faction, two arenas, two bosses, three music tracks.** Five
   modes all play in the same two places against the same skeletons. That is
   where "repetitive" comes from, more than the rules.
8. **No reason to log in tomorrow beyond bounties.** There is no daily first
   win, login streak, season or mastery track, and nothing that is visibly
   *about to happen*.
9. **Builds are shallow at the card layer.** Most runs end with
   might/haste/crit plus one class card. Evolutions exist but are rare (the
   autopilot reached one in 40 runs).
10. **No social layer at all.** There are no leaderboards, friends or ghosts.
    With no backend that is fine for now, but Game Center leaderboards are
    nearly free on iOS.

## 1. Difficulty and pacing (the evidence)

Barrow, Horde Hunt, two runs per cell.

| Hero | Normal, Lv 1 | Veteran, Lv 1 | Veteran, Lv 10 | Nightmare, Lv 10 | Nightmare, Lv 20 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Knight | 2/2 won, 75–91 s | 2/2 | 2/2 | 1/2 | 2/2 |
| Barbarian | 2/2, 89–90 s | 2/2 | 2/2 | 0/2 (boss at 75–80%) | 0/2 (boss 15% / 77%) |
| Mage | 2/2, 68–71 s | 1/2 | 2/2 | 0/2, **died before boss** at ~45 s | 0/2, **died before boss** |
| Ranger | 2/2, 114–195 s | 0/2 | 1/2 | 0/2, **died before boss** at ~42 s | 0/2, one before boss |

What this says:

- **Hunts are short.** The boss wakes at 52–62 s on every tier. The README
  promises 2–3 minutes; Normal is closer to 75 s. The soul phase is the same
  length on every tier, so Nightmare is not a longer fight, just a spikier one.
- **Normal's boss has no teeth.** At 3,400 HP he dies before he shows his
  enrage. A new player never learns the telegraphs, because they never need to.
- **The tier jump is a cliff.** Foe HP goes ×1 → ×2.6 → ×6.5 and damage
  ×1 → ×2.1 → ×4.6. A hero level is worth +9% (later +4%). Level 20 is about
  +130% over level 1, which barely dents a ×6.5 / ×4.6 jump. Gear and talents
  are meant to close that gap, but the player can't see how far off they are.
- **Ranged heroes are punished by crowd density, not damage.** At Nightmare,
  Mage and Ranger die in the soul phase while kiting, when packs spawn ahead of
  them ("pack waiting ahead") and they get surrounded. Melee heroes live
  because they stand in it.

**Fixes**

- **Normal:** about +25% boss HP. The first boss of a player's life should
  enrage. Raise the soul goal from 110 to about 150 so a hunt is 2 minutes, and
  make the first surge arrive earlier.
- **Smooth the tiers.** Add a tier between each (Normal I/II/III style, or
  "Torment 1–6" like Diablo 3). Each step is +40–60%, not ×2.5. Players get a
  new tier to unlock every few sessions instead of hitting one wall.
- **Recommended power** on the expedition card ("Recommended 1,400 · You 1,120")
  so a loss reads as "I need gear", not "this is unfair".
- **Ranged:** a knockback on the Ranger's dash-out and fewer
  spawn-ahead packs while the hero is below 30% HP. Give kiting a way out,
  don't remove the pressure.
- **Scale the soul phase on higher tiers** (goal ×1.15/tier) so harder means
  longer *and* harder, and add a second champion wave on Nightmare.

## 2. Progression and economy

| Currency | Comes from | Goes to | Problem |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gold | Every hunt (~300–600 Normal, ~1,900 Nightmare), salvage, bounties, road | Hero upgrades only | Hero 1→10 costs 11,480 gold, about 30 Normal wins. Nothing else to buy, so gold piles up (1,240 sitting in the camp screenshot). |
| Shards | Chests (10/18/30, split 60/40 between **two random heroes of four**), road, two bounties | Hero upgrades, unlocks | Expected ~2.5 shards per hero per Normal chest. Level 1→10 needs 628, about 250 Normal wins for the hero you play. Level 10→20 needs 5,400 more. |
| Skill XP | Damage dealt | Gates gear tiers | Works, and is the best long-term hook (OSRS curve). |
| Renown | Every hunt | Road rewards | Good. The road's empty bar looks broken on a new save (see 4). |

**Fixes**

- **Shards go to the hero you played** (Brawl Stars "power points"). Keep a
  small random split for unlocking others. That alone cuts the 1→10 grind
  about 3×.
- **Gold sinks that make you stronger and that you choose:**
  - **Reforge:** pay gold to reroll one affix on an item.
  - **Upgrade an item's material** (Bronze → Iron) with gold and salvage.
  - **Talent respec** costs gold after the first free one.
  - **Cosmetics:** weapon glows and cape dyes, bought with gold.
- **Make level 11–20 matter:** each level past 10 should also raise a visible
  cap (an extra card choice at level 15, an extra potion charge at 20), not
  only +4%.
- **Gear sets** (already in PLAN "Next up 8"): the Gravemaw uniques as a
  2/3-piece set and the Hexer uniques as another. Sets give a reason to farm one
  boss on purpose.

## 3. Variety: why it gets repetitive

Content count: 2 zones, 2 bosses, about 10 foe types (all skeletons), 4 heroes,
38 cards, 9 champion affixes, 5 modes, 3 music tracks.

The modes are good rules, but they share **the same two arenas and one
faction**. After an hour the player has seen every enemy silhouette. Rule
variety without *visual and audio* variety reads as "the same thing again".

**Fixes, by cost**

- **Cheap:**
  - Per-mode arena dressing: fog color, light and props on the existing two
    maps, so Descent at depth 10 looks different from depth 1.
  - Music per mode (Descent should get more intense with depth).
  - More boss voice lines.
- **Medium:**
  - A third zone using the KayKit forest/Halloween packs already imported,
    with a non-skeleton faction (pumpkin men, bats, ghouls) and its own boss.
  - Mini-bosses in the soul phase (a named Bone Brute with one telegraphed
    move).
  - Rooms with a twist on the Long Road: a darkness room, a cursed altar room,
    a treasure vault with a timer.
- **Card depth:**
  - Raise evolution odds once both parts are held (guarantee the offer at the
    next level-up).
  - Show evolution recipes in the Log.
  - Add 2–3 "build-defining" legendaries per hero that change a rule, not a
    number. For example: the Ranger's arrows ricochet, or the Knight blocks
    frontal hits while standing still.
  - Add **card banishes** (remove a card from this run's pool, three per run)
    so players steer builds.

**Camp**

- A fresh Knight wears a tunic and a dagger. Give every hero a starter outfit
  that reads as its class: helmet and sword for the Knight, hood and bow for
  the Ranger. The bare look should never appear on the home screen.
- The Renown Road card shows an empty bar on a new save, which looks like a
  loading bug. Show "0 / 60" and the next reward's icon, and start the bar with
  the first hunt's renown.
- The hero stands in the middle third, but the lower third is a dark band.
  Bring the camera down a little or add a ground prop so the scene fills the
  frame.

**Hunt**

- The HUD is clean after Phase 9. The hero HP bar shows only a number ("620"),
  so there is no sense of *how hurt*. Use the bar fill as the main read, keep
  the number small.
- In the death shot a foreground rock covers the bottom third of the screen,
  and on the boss shot the gallows hides Gravemaw's body. Fade any prop
  between camera and hero/boss (dither to 30%), or keep tall props out of the
  camera-side band.

**Victory**

- Loot labels are about 10 px tall and overlap each other by the crypt, far
  from the hero. Make labels short and big ("Rare Helm"), stack them with
  spacing, and pull loot toward the hero (a vacuum on victory).
- The combat buttons stay on screen after the boss dies. Hide them, slow time
  for half a second on the killing blow, zoom in on the boss's collapse, then
  play the gold fountain.

**Results**

- No **Play again** button. Every Supercell game puts it next to "Return".
  Each extra tap between runs costs runs.
- Loot rows show Ranger/Mage items with a green ▲ and "Equip" while playing
  the Knight. Name the hero ("▲ Mage"), or put the other heroes' upgrades under
  one line.
- Skill rows show "+184 xp" with no bar. Show a thin bar filling toward the
  next level; an OSRS player wants to see it move.
- The bottom half is empty, and the chest is a single text line. Show the
  chest model or icon big, and show road progress filling there (renown +37 →
  bar moves).

**Everywhere**

- Add a **first-hunt tutorial beat** beyond the one hint: the first champion,
  shrine and pact each get a one-time callout.
- Numbers count up with sound (gold, renown, XP). The plan says count-ups
  exist on results; extend them to camp after returning (gold ticks up on the
  top bar).

## 5. Retention: what brings people back

| Already there | Missing |
| --- | --- |
| Daily bounties (3) | **First win of the day** bonus (double chest) |
| Renown road | **Login streak** (7-day calendar, small rewards, a hero skin on day 7) |
| Weekly omen | **Season** (a 4–6 week renown track with a cosmetic finale; the road resets visually, not the rewards already earned) |
| Clue scrolls | **Hero mastery** per hero (kills/wins → titles, weapon glows, a portrait frame) |
| Collection log | **Achievements** with Game Center sync |
| Descent best depth | **Leaderboards** (Game Center): Descent depth, Boss Rush time, weekly omen score |
| | **Offline/idle reward** at camp: the fire collects a little gold while you're away; a reason to open the app |
| | **"Almost there" hooks**: the camp shows the closest goal ("2 wins to Veteran", "12 shards to Lv 6") |

The single most effective of these for a grind game is **hero mastery plus a
season track**. Both turn every hunt into visible progress on something.

## 6. Game feel and audio

Hitstop, shake, haptics, damage-number merging and audio buses are all in.
The gaps:

- **Three music tracks.** Add a Descent/deep-tier track, a victory sting that
  rises into the camp theme, and a low-HP music filter (muffle under 25% HP).
- **The killing blow on a boss** should be a set piece: slow motion, a white
  flash, a unique sound. Today it is a normal hit.
- **Crits** need a distinct sound and a bigger number. **Level-up** needs a
  short radial burst before the cards appear.
- **Champion death** should drop a visible "loot fountain" even when it
  rolls nothing good: gold coins popping out sell the moment.

## 7. Bigger ideas (the mix the README names)

- **Hunt Royale:** a **2-player co-op** hunt (local or online later) is the
  game's most-requested genre feature. The Wilderness rival AI is a start; a
  "bot ally" hero picked from your roster is a cheap step toward it.
- **Diablo:**
  - **Rift keys:** Descent past depth 10 drops a key that opens a harder,
    shorter rift with a guaranteed legendary.
  - **Ancient items:** a 1% roll with +30% stats and an orange border.
- **OSRS:**
  - **Boss kill counts** in the Log ("Gravemaw KC 214").
  - **Pets per boss.** Only Gravemaw has one; add the Hexer's.
  - A **"dry streak"** counter on the collection log.
  - All three cost very little and are pure grind fuel.
- **Supercell:**
  - **Trophy-style rating per hero:** wins add, losses subtract, and milestones
    pay out. That turns every hunt into stakes without new content.

**Start with 11.1–11.5.** They are small, they touch what every player sees in
their first ten minutes, and the autopilot can verify 11.1 and 11.2. After
that, 11.7 (the tier ladder) is the most important balance change. 11.14 (the
third zone) is the most important content change.

**Not decided here, for you:** monetization. Nothing in the game charges money
today. If the game stays premium (paid or free with no IAP), the season track
should be free. If not, the season track is where a pass would go. Decide that
before building 11.16.
