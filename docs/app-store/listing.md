## Description

Gravelight is a fast one-thumb action RPG. Drag to move, your hero fights on its own, and you pick the upgrades that make each hunt different.

- Three heroes to start (knight, ranger, mage) and more to unlock on the Trophy Road.
- Hunt the Barrow, the Sunken Crypt and the Grove. Beat the bosses and open their chests.
- Hatch eggs, raise sidekicks, breed rarer ones and show off your best three.
- A new weird-rules event every week, a story campaign, outfits for every hero.
- Join a clan, climb the rankings and raid together. Everything works offline too.
- No ads. No pay to win.

## App Review notes

Gravelight needs no sign-in. Online features create an anonymous account on first launch after the privacy screen. To see them: Camp > Clan. Account deletion: Clan > Account, cloud save and new phone > Delete my account. Crash reporting is off unless the reviewer turns it on in Settings > Privacy.
