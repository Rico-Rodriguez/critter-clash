# Gravelight privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-26_

Gravelight is a single-player action game with optional online features. This page says what the app stores and sends.

## What stays on your phone

Your progress (heroes, gear, pets, settings) is saved on the device. A local error log (`errors.json`) records script errors and whether the last session ended unexpectedly. It never leaves the phone unless you tap **Copy report** or opt in to crash reports below.

## Online play (on by default, can be turned off)

When online play is on, the game creates an anonymous account on our game server. It uses:

- a random key generated on your device (not your Apple ID, advertising ID or any hardware ID)
- the hunter name you choose (filtered for bad words and contact details)
- game progress used for rankings, clans and parties: power, trophies, clan and party membership, clan chat messages, and an optional cloud copy of your save

We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and we do not show ads or use tracking SDKs. Data is not sold or shared with advertisers.

You can turn online play off at any time in **Settings > Privacy**; it takes effect on the next launch. You can delete your online account and its data in the game (Clan > Account, cloud save and new phone > **Delete my account**), which removes the account, its cloud save, rankings and clan membership from the server.

## Crash reports (off by default)

If you switch on **Share crash reports**, then after a crash the next launch sends: the game and Godot version, the iOS version, the device model (for example `iPhone15,2`), session and crash counts, and the logged script errors (file, line, message). It contains no save data, name, account ID or advertising ID. You can switch this off at any time in Settings. Reports go to our crash-reporting provider and are kept for at most 90 days.

## Children

Gravelight is rated for ages 9+ (fantasy violence). It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Chat is limited to clans and filtered.
