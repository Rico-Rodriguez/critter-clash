# The town, the character select and the two ways out

- 2026-10-21 — **The character select is the front door.** The game opens on it: the six classes, the one you are looking at standing on its plinth in 3D wearing what you equipped it with, and ENTER THE TOWN takes that hero into the world — `scripts/char_select.gd`, `scripts/app.gd` (`_ready`)
- 2026-10-21 — The roster tile is shared, so a hero reads the same in the character select and in the Heroes tab — `scripts/hero_tile.gd`, `scripts/hero_screen.gd`
- 2026-10-21 — **The town has two ways out, not four gates.** The town wall has a gate north and a gate south: the north barrier leads to the first depth that is not cleared yet (the deepest one once they all are) and the south barrier to the shallow ground. The north barrier opens when the Fringe is cleared — `scripts/world_data.gd` (`town_gates`), `scripts/hub_scene.gd` (`_gates`), `scripts/app.gd` (`_ways_out`, `_way_row`, `_travel`)
- 2026-10-21 — The town is a place now: a wall of standing stone round it with a gap at each gate, a well in the plaza, and the critters who keep it (a stall keeper at each stall, one by the fire, one at the gate) — `scripts/hub_scene.gd` (`_wall`, `_well`, `_people`)
- 2026-10-21 — The town is small enough that both barriers are in the picture: the camera stands outside the south gate and looks north over the plaza, and a tap on either standing stone walks the hero through it — `scripts/hub_view.gd` (`CAM_POS`, `CAM_LOOK`), `scripts/hub_scene.gd`
- 2026-10-21 — **One door per depth, each leading to the biome that depth is made of.** The Meadow, the Mushroom, the Frost and the Ember gates stand in an arc across the far wall of the town, left to right in the order of the depths, and each opens when the one before it is cleared — `scripts/world_data.gd` (`town_gates`), `scripts/hub_scene.gd` (`_gates`, `DOOR_ARC`)
- 2026-10-21 — The town camera has a wide lens (62°) because the town is 20 m across and a portrait frame at 46° only ever showed the middle of the plaza — `scripts/hub_view.gd`
- 2026-10-21 — **The starters are always unlocked.** The catalog calls them starters, and the character select is the front door, so a save that lost its roster (or never had one) still has the four to choose from; only the two road heroes stay locked — `scripts/save_game.gd` (`_sanitize`)
- 2026-10-21 — The plinth camera was a close-up of a torso (the hero filled 60% of the frame at a 30° lens); it now frames the whole hero at 42% and 34° — `scripts/stage3d.gd` (`_frame_on`)
- 2026-10-21 — The stalls are places in the town now: a tap on one opens its trade sheet, so the counter keeps the ways out, the desk and the daily — `scripts/hub_scene.gd` (`_places`), `scripts/app.gd` (`_open_place`)
- 2026-10-21 — **Feedback on every tap.** A shut door says which ground has to be cleared first, and a place that opens nothing says what it is — `scripts/app.gd` (`_travel`, `_open_place`)
- 2026-10-21 — The town's counter was overflowing the screen (the service row sat 31 px below it); the town gives the counter more room and the way rows are shorter — `scripts/app.gd` (`CAMP_FRACTION`, `_way_row`)
- 2026-10-21 — The places of the town: the forge (an anvil on its block), the slayer master's post (a skull on a post), the trophy board and the stash chest stand where they are in the town, and a tap on one opens the screen that belongs to it — `scripts/hub_scene.gd` (`_places`), `scripts/hub_view.gd` (`place_at`, `place_tapped`), `scripts/app.gd`
- 2026-10-21 — The plan for the whole flow is written down: `docs/the-shape-of-the-game.md`
