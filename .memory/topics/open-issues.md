# Open issues

Each entry says what is wrong, where it is, and the next step. Nothing here is
finished work.

- 2026-10-21 — **The run still plays the old whole-world map.** The per-tier `ZoneBuilder` zones exist and are checked by `tools/zone_check.gd`, but `RunSim` builds `HuntMap.world`, so a tier hunt is the old map with tier-scaled monsters. Next step: build the zone for the tier and let the run play it; the stage ground grid is currently hard-wired to `HuntMap.WORLD_ROWS/WORLD_COLS`.
- 2026-10-21 — **The bot cannot clear the meadow.** The full sim-check ends in seven failures, all of them the meadow bot fights: sorceress, rogue and barbarian each lose the meadow at exactly 119.0 s (outside the 2.5–5.5 min window the check wants) and the full seeded bot run ends in a loss. These failures pre-date this work (they failed with ember, juniper and pebble as well). What we know: 119 s is the shared hunt clock (`HUNT_LIMIT`), the lair boss rises at 68 s (`EXPLORE_BOSS_AT`), and the whole-world map is 4480×8960 sim px, so a fresh hero has 51 s to fight a boss it has to reach on foot. Next step: decide whether the meadow is too hard for a fresh hero, the walk is too long, or the bot steering is too passive — and note that the per-tier zones are 2560 px tall instead of 8960, which would also shorten that walk.
- 2026-10-21 — **The whole-world hunt is the old flow and nothing plays it now.** The daily and the seeded gear runs moved onto the zone flow, and the bot fights play the fringe zone. What is left of the world map is the daily's map data and the map checks. Next step: retire `HuntMap.world` once the daily and the map sheet are gone, or keep it as the daily's ground on purpose and say so in the docs.
- 2026-10-21 — **The README still describes the old game.** It opens with "from home, open a biome", lists the old critter ids as the playable cast and describes only three roles (Mage, Archer, Tank), while the playable roster is now barbarian, paladin, sorceress, rogue, necromancer, druid and the camp is the front door. Next step: rewrite the Play section once the camp flow is settled.
- 2026-10-21 — The camp shows the hero by the fire, but the counter still names him in words; a portrait or a small 3D echo of the hero in the YOUR HERO row would make the choice read at a glance. Next step: a small hero portrait in the row (the sprite bank has no class art yet).
